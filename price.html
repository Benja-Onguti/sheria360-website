FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Do you offer a free trial?
Absolutely. All paid plans come with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. You get full access to all features.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Yes, you save 20% when you choose yearly billing. The discount is applied automatically at checkout.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and PayPal. Enterprise plans can also be invoiced.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Sheria360 uses bank-grade AES-256 encryption, SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure, and role-based access controls to keep your data safe.
