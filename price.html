Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Sheria360 cost?
Sheria360 is priced per user, per month. The Core plan is KES 2,500 per user/month, Professional is KES 7,500 per user/month, and Enterprise is KES 15,000 per user/month. All plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.
Is Sheria360 priced per user or per firm?
Sheria360 is priced per user, per month. This means you only pay for the team members who need access, making it scalable for firms of any size — from a 2-person practice to a large multi-office legal department.
Is there a free trial available?
Yes, all paid plans come with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. You get full access to all features so you can see exactly how the platform works for your firm before committing.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and prorated charges will apply if you are upgrading.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Yes, you save up to 20% when you choose yearly billing compared to our monthly plans. The discount is applied automatically at checkout.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and PayPal. For our Enterprise plans, invoicing and bank transfers are also available.
Are there any hidden fees or long-term contracts?
No. Sheria360 has no hidden fees and no long-term contracts. You can cancel or change your plan at any time. All prices are transparent and inclusive of the features listed in your chosen plan.
Is my client data secure?
Security is our top priority. Sheria360 uses bank-grade AES-256 encryption, operates on SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure, and implements strict role-based access controls to keep your sensitive legal data safe.
Can I pay in Kenyan Shillings (KES)?
Yes, all pricing is listed in Kenyan Shillings (KES) per user, per month. We also accept payments in USD for international clients. Contact us for multi-currency options.
Do you offer custom pricing for large teams?
Yes, for teams larger than 10 users or organizations with specific requirements, we offer custom Enterprise pricing. Please contact our sales team for a tailored quote.
