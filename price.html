Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and prorated charges will apply if you are upgrading.
Do you offer a free trial?
Absolutely. All paid plans come with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. You get full access to all features so you can see exactly how the platform works for your firm.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Yes, you save up to 20% when you choose yearly billing compared to our monthly plans. The discount is applied automatically at checkout.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and PayPal. For our Enterprise plans, invoicing and bank transfers are also available.
Is my client data secure?
Security is our top priority. Sheria360 uses bank-grade AES-256 encryption, operates on SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure, and implements strict role-based access controls to keep your sensitive legal data safe.
