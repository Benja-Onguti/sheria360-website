External Counsel Portal

AI enhanced
Dedicated collaboration space for external legal partners. Sheria360's External Counsel Portal streamlines engagement with outside counsel. From matter assignment and document sharing to time tracking and billing, this portal provides a secure, transparent, and efficient environment for external legal collaboration. Reduce administrative overhead and improve case outcomes through better communication and real-time visibility.
Matter Assignment & Access
Securely assign matters to external counsel with controlled access to case files, documents, and relevant information.
Document Management & Sharing
Share, upload, and collaborate on documents with external counsel using version control and complete audit trails.
Time Tracking & Reporting
External counsel can log time against matters with automated reporting and integration to billing systems.
Fee Notes & Invoice Submission
Submit fee notes and invoices directly through the portal with status tracking and automated approval workflows.
Secure Communication Hub
Encrypted messaging and comment system for matter-specific discussions, reducing email clutter and improving response times.
Task & Deadline Management
Assign and track tasks for external counsel with automated reminders and deadline notifications.
Matter Progress Monitoring
Real-time visibility into matter progress with updates, milestones, and status indicators shared with external counsel.
Court & ADR Session Coordination
Coordinate court appearances and ADR sessions with external counsel, including calendar integration and conflict checks.
Collaboration on Legal Strategy
Shared workspace for developing case strategies, sharing insights, and aligning on legal approaches with external partners.
Confidentiality & Access Controls
Granular permissions ensure external counsel only sees information relevant to their assigned matters, maintaining client confidentiality.
AI-Assisted Research & Drafting
External counsel can leverage Sheria360 AI for legal research, document drafting, and summarization within the portal.
Comprehensive Audit Trail
Full audit log of all actions, document access, and communications with external counsel for compliance and transparency.
